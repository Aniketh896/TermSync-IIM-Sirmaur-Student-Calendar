## 3 · Your data

- TermSync runs **inside your own Google account**, with the permissions you approve
  on Google's consent screen. It only creates, changes and removes events in the one
  calendar it creates for you (named after the term, e.g. "IIM Sirmaur – Term 2"),
  and never reads or changes the events in your other calendars. It reads your
  email address only to know who you are; it never reads your email.
- Your name, roll number and section come from the batch roster your maintainer
  added. Your course selection and preferences are stored in your own Google account.
- Unless your maintainer switched it off, TermSync records when you enable, sync or
  stop it, and any "wrong entry" report you send. Your maintainer and the SAC team
  can see these in TermSync's admin panel and in hidden tabs of the batch's
  spreadsheet.
- You can stop at any time: **Stop Auto-Sync…** on the page, and you can delete the
  TermSync calendar in Google Calendar whenever you like.

## 4 · For maintainers

- You run your batch's copy from your own institute account and are responsible for
  how it is set up and shared. Keep your filled-in `config.gs` (it lists your
  batchmates' names and roll numbers) out of any public place.
- Keep the credits — "Created by Aniketh Hotagi", and the SAC — in the app and its
  documents, as the [licence](LICENSE) requires the copyright notice to be kept.
- Tell your batch, when you share the link, that it's unofficial and that the
  official timetable comes first. The suggested message in
  [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md) says so.
