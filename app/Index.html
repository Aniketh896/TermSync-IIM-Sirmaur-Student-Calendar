    Applies the latest timetable the tool has read from the institute (it re-reads it every 30 minutes) and puts back anything missing from your calendar — without waiting for the next automatic update.
    ℹ️ How it works
    Your courses are filled in for you from the institute's records — just confirm them (or adjust the selection) and click Enable Auto-Sync. The tool then checks the institute timetable and the quiz, mid-term & end-term schedules every half hour and keeps your Google Calendar updated when changes are found. Quizzes (📝, red) and exams — both Mid-Term and End-Term (📕, blue) — for your selected courses are added automatically in standout colours so they stand out from regular classes. Once synced, you can also download your full schedule as a PDF 📲 for offline use — and you can close this page anytime.
    ⚠️ Unofficial tool — verify against the official timetable
    The official timetable from the institute administration is the only source of truth. Please verify your schedule there before relying on these reminders. The creator is not responsible for missed classes, incorrect entries, scheduling conflicts, or any other issue arising from use of this tool. Use at your own discretion.
