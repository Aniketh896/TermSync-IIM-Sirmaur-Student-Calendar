# Code of conduct

TermSync is built by students for students. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull
requests and discussions — is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to code;
- give and take feedback about the work, never the person;
- protect other students' privacy: no names, roll numbers or personal details of
  anyone who hasn't agreed to it;
- keep discussion on topic and free of harassment of any kind.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Problems can be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
